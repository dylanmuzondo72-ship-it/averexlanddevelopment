ABOUT AVEREX

Professional guidance across the property journey

Averex Land Solutions is a Zimbabwean land and property consultancy focused on helping clients investigate opportunities, reduce avoidable risk and coordinate development work with greater clarity.

Our role is to connect the right information, professionals and project steps so clients can move from an initial land enquiry to a better-informed decision and a more organised implementation process.

01

Evidence before commitment
Review the available documents, site context and professional input before major decisions.

02

One coordinated process
Bring together technical, administrative and development activities around a clear plan.

03

Value-focused thinking
Approach land as a long-term asset that needs planning, protection and proper management.

OUR SERVICES

Property support built around informed action

Every engagement is shaped by the property, available records and the client’s objective. No mysterious one-size-fits-all ritual involving a clipboard and optimism.

01

Land & Property Advisory

Guidance for buyers, sellers, investors and developers evaluating land or property opportunities.

  • Opportunity assessment
  • Transaction guidance
  • Development considerations
02

Property Due Diligence

Structured review of available property information before acquisition, investment or development.

  • Document review support
  • Risk and issue identification
  • Professional referrals where required
03

Ownership Verification Support

Assistance coordinating checks on ownership information and property documentation through relevant channels.

  • Title-document checks
  • Ownership information review
  • Record and stakeholder coordination
04

Site & Development Assessment

Practical review of the physical site and its implications for planning and development.

  • Site inspection coordination
  • Boundary and survey support
  • Access and infrastructure review

Project Management

  • Scope and programme coordination

  • Progress monitoring and reporting

Development Coordination

HOW WE WORK

A clear route from enquiry to action

Property work becomes expensive when nobody agrees what is being checked, who is responsible, or what happens next. We prefer the revolutionary idea of a defined process.

01

Consult

We establish the property, the intended transaction or development goal, and the decisions you need to make.

02

Investigate

We identify the required documents, site information, stakeholders and specialist checks.

03

Advise

We organise the findings into practical guidance, issues to resolve and recommended next steps.

04

Coordinate

Where required, we support the project through consultant coordination, monitoring and implementation oversight.
